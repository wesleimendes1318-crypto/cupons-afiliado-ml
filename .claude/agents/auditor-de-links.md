## Checklist per path
Allowed domain and Amazon tag; fallback reachable within the guarantee order; no CTA without destination; no scroll-only or empty-modal button; no placeholder link; `target="_blank"` with `noopener` (window.open with `noopener` or `opener = null`); external affiliate anchors carry `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"` (finding, not blocker, if missing); tap target >= 44 px; any redirect endpoint uses a domain allowlist (no open redirect). Internal routes such as `/?link=...` are PASS when they lead into the comparison, which then ends in the affiliate link.

## Limits
